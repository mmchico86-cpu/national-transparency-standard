MEASURABLE ACCOUNTABILITY

What NTS measures

Existing systems often track activity. NTS is designed to measure the quality, traceability, and durability of the response process itself.

Response

Were the material issues actually acknowledged and substantively answered?

Verification

Can the response be traced to supporting records and an identifiable basis?

Resolution

Was the issue documented as resolved, or merely administratively closed?

Accountability

Can referrals, corrections, decisions, and review history be reconstructed?

Trust & Security

Are records protected with access controls, audit history, integrity checks, and transparent governance?

Time & Repeat Burden

How many days passed, how often did the issue repeat, and how long did the unresolved burden continue?

DIFFERENTIATORS

Designed around the gaps advocates already fight.

Historical Baseline

Reconstruct prior matters so improvement starts from a documented baseline instead of a marketing claim.

Verification Integrity

Distinguish “status verified” from evidence reviewed, supporting basis located, and disputed issues actually addressed.

Proof Without Postage

Create a digital submission and delivery trail with timestamps, custody states, and evidence-integrity controls.

Evidence Integrity

SHA-256 hashing, original-file preservation, version history, and immutable audit events help detect silent alteration.

Case Health

Days open, last response, unresolved issues, deadlines, exposure, and the next meaningful action in one view.

Exposure Risk

Surface accumulating operational, compliance, reputational, and consumer-impact risk without pretending to be a legal-liability score.
