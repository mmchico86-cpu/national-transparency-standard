5-RECORD NTS BASELINE — NEXT STEPS

Payment complete? Prepare your five redacted records.

This page explains what to prepare after purchasing the 5-Record NTS Baseline Assessment. Do not send sensitive files through this public website. After payment, you will receive access to a private submission folder before any records are transferred.

What to prepare

1
Choose five historical mattersClosed or substantially complete matters are easiest to reconstruct and compare.
2
Include the complaint or original issueProvide the document, message, form, or record showing what was originally raised.
3
Include the response trailResponses, referrals, verification notices, closure notices, corrective actions, and relevant follow-ups.
4
Redact unnecessary personal dataKeep enough context to understand the matter, but remove sensitive identifiers that are not needed for the review.
5
Keep each matter togetherUse a simple folder or file naming format such as Matter-01, Matter-02, through Matter-05.

What the baseline review returns

A
TracePoint evidence chainMaterial Issue → Organizational Response → Supporting Source → Verified Finding → Resolution Status.
B
Five-pillar NTS baselineResponse, Verification, Resolution, Accountability, and Trust & Security observations where supported.
C
Verification-integrity findingsDistinguishes status repetition from a documented supporting basis and material issue review.
D
Case health and time metricsDays open, response gaps, unresolved periods, repeat burden, and other measurable timing indicators.
E
Improvement pathA concise source-linked summary of strengths, gaps, and the next process improvements to consider.
Do not include:
  • Social Security numbers
  • Full bank, card, or financial account numbers
  • Passwords, access codes, or authentication secrets
  • Unnecessary medical information
  • Unnecessary dates of birth, driver-license numbers, or other identity data
  • Any information you are not authorized to provide
